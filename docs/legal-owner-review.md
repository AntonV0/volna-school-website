# Policy Migration Status

Public-safe record of the policy pages approved on the original Volna School
website and migrated into this rebuild. This file is not legal advice and must
not contain private contracts, customer information, payment records, or
reviewer-only comments.

## Approved source

The published pages on `volnaschool.com` were audited in English and Russian in
August 2026. Their final public copy is the authoritative source for the
rebuild:

- Privacy Policy — English and Russian
- Lesson Terms, Cancellations and Refunds — English and Russian
- Safeguarding and Online Safety Policy — English and Russian

The migration preserves the published wording, section order, internal policy
links, contact details, cancellation form, SEO titles and descriptions, social
metadata, and indexing choices.

## Search behaviour

- Privacy and Refund pages intentionally use `noindex, follow` and are omitted
  from the sitemap.
- Safeguarding pages are indexable, are included in the sitemap, and expose
  reciprocal English, Russian, and `x-default` alternates.

## Ongoing owner responsibility

Re-review the policies whenever the school's services, contact details,
providers, payment arrangements, safeguarding procedures, legal obligations,
or data-processing practices materially change. Update both language versions
and their displayed review dates together where appropriate.
